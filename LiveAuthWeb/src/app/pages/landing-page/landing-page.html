FAQ

Do users need an account?

No. They pay a Lightning invoice to prove human intent — no signup required.

Is this a CAPTCHA replacement?

Yes — with economic friction instead of frustrating puzzles.

What about privacy?

Zero tracking. Zero fingerprinting. Zero PII stored.

Can I customize the cost?

Yes — set sats per endpoint and per project.

What if Lightning fails?

Proof-of-Work always works. Lightning is the fallback for slow/mobile devices.

Do I need a Lightning node?

No — LiveAuth handles all Lightning infrastructure for you.
